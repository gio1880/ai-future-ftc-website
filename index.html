BIOBUZZ 2026–27

BIOBUZZ is inspired by biodiversity and the natural world. Two alliances of two robots collect game pieces and score them in hives and flowers: Pollen (balls 71 mm across) and the bigger Nectar (91 mm).

Our robot has one intake that sorts the two balls by size, and a shooter for each. See where the robot is now.
