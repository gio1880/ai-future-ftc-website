The machine

CR1 v6 scores Pollen. It picks balls off the floor and out of the flower, lines them up in a lane, and fires them with topspin from a turret.

01

Topspin shooter

One printed head: flywheel on top, grippy 55° ramp under the ball, servo gate that holds each shot until we fire.

How it shoots →
02

Turret + camera

Two servos swing the head ±90°. The camera rides on top so the wedge never blocks its view of the field tags.

How we aim →
03

Intake + lane

A tubing roller grabs every ball. A 4-ball lane with powered wheels keeps balls moving up to the shooter.

Notebook entries →
04

Flower wedge

A low wedge slides under the flower's collar so Pollen rolls out to the intake. Three shapes ready to test.

Notebook entries →
05

Closed-loop code

Speed control on the intake and flywheel, a jam detector, a feeder that waits for full flywheel speed, odometry.

Control →
06

Made in-house

All 12 plywood plates from one 2 × 4 ft sheet on our laser cutter, printed parts on our Bambu printers, metal with our sponsors.
