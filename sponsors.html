PCBWay

A 50% sponsorship credit on our custom metal parts. Their engineers also reviewed our files and found a 0.10 mm sliver at the front cover's switch opening, which we fixed.

pcbway.com

goBILDA

A 25% FTC team discount on goBILDA parts. Our drivetrain runs on goBILDA motors, and the Pinpoint odometry computer tells the robot where it is in autonomous.
